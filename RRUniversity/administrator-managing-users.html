Manage the Team

Add and remove RapidReconciler team members, assign each person a role, and choose which databases and companies they can work in. When you invite someone who signs in with a password, RapidReconciler emails them a secure link to set their own. Someone on a single sign-on domain gets no email: they sign in through your identity provider.

What changed

Earlier versions configured each team member with a per-person grid of functions, tabs, and companies. RapidReconciler now uses named roles instead: you pick one role, and it sets the view the person works in. Then, for each database, you choose the modules and companies they can use.

Where to find it

Team member management lives on the RR Team page. On the RapidReconciler Home page, find the Team & Access card. Its first row shows your member and role count, and its second row shows the state of your access review. Click either one to open RR Team. (The third row opens Complex Passwords.)

The Team & Access card is on Home’s administrator view, which Administrators and Power Users see. Anyone else who opens the RR Team page gets the message This is an administrator function. Contact your administrator to manage team members.

The RR Team page

The page header holds the title, two contact fields under it, and the New Team Member button on the right. Project Sponsor and RR Administrator record the email addresses of the people who own the RapidReconciler relationship at your site. There is no Save button: a change is saved when you click or tab out of the field.

Below the header are the access review band and the Team Members grid, which shows a count of team members and an Export button in its title bar. The grid lists everyone who can sign in to RapidReconciler, one row per person:

ColumnWhat it shows
ActiveA badge reading Active or Inactive: whether the account can currently sign in.
Full NameThe person's name.
UsernameTheir email address — this is also how they sign in.
RoleThe single role assigned to the team member (for example Analyst).
Sign-inHow the person signs in: Password, or your identity provider (for example Entra). See App sign-in.
Last LoginWhen the team member most recently signed in.
Last Pass ChangeWhen the team member last set or changed their password.
OptionsA lock icon (Permissions: role and access), a pencil icon (Edit team member), and a trash icon (Delete team member).

Access review and the access report

The band above the grid records a periodic sign-off that your team list is correct. Its headline reads Access review needed (never reviewed), Access review overdue, or Access review up to date, with who last reviewed it and when the next review is due.

  1. Check the team list in the grid.
  2. Tick I’ve reviewed the team list and it’s correct.
  3. Under Remind me in, choose 30 days, 60 days or 90 days.
  4. Click Record review. It stays disabled until the box is ticked.

Each review is recorded with your name, the date and the member count. The access review row on Home’s Team & Access card shows the same state.

Click Export (the download arrow in the Team Members title bar) to download an Excel workbook named rr-access-report- plus today’s date. It has two sheets:

  • Access Report: each team member with their sign-in method, role, active state, last sign-in and password-change dates, and one row per database they can use with its company access.
  • Access Review Log: every recorded access review, with who reviewed, the member count, the reminder cadence and the next due date.

Adding a team member

To add someone, click the New Team Member button in the top-right of the RR Team page. The New team member form opens. Fill in the fields and click Send invite:

FieldDescription
Full NameThe person's first and last name. Required.
EmailTheir company email address. This is their username, and it is where the set-password link is sent.
App sign-inFills in as you type the email: how this address signs in, decided by its domain. On a single sign-on domain it also offers the bypass box. See App sign-in.
RoleThe single role that sets which view this person works in. It starts on Analyst, and choosing a role shows a short description of what it grants. Only roles that grant a module your company is licensed for are offered. See Roles below.
DatabasesCheck each RapidReconciler database the team member may sign in to. For each one, tick the modules they may open and choose All licensed companies or check individual companies. See Database, module & company access.

The person appears on the RR Team list right away. A message at the bottom of the page says what happened to the invite:

  • For someone who signs in with a password, RapidReconciler emails a single-use, time-limited link to set their own password. They can sign in once they have set it.
  • For someone on a single sign-on domain without the bypass, no email is sent, because there is no password to set. They sign in through your identity provider.
  • If the email address is refused, the person is still added but no email goes out. The message asks you to contact RapidReconciler support.

Roles

A role decides which view of RapidReconciler a person works in. There are four, and each of the first three is its own view only: an Administrator does not also get the Analyst or Accountant view.

RoleWhat they see
AdministratorThe administrator view of Home: the Licensing, Team & Access, Activity Log and Troubleshooting cards and the pages they open. No analyst or accountant work.
AnalystThe analyst view: Data Health, Transaction Variance, Cardex Variance, the Tools shelf and the Audit Center, and the actions that fix what they find.
AccountantThe accountant view: Overview, Accounts, On Hand and the Audit Center.
Power UserAll three views, with a switcher to move between them. Use it for someone who both administers RapidReconciler and does the reconciliation work.
One role per team member

Each person has exactly one role, and every team member must have one. To change what someone can do, change their role. Only Administrators and Power Users can manage the RR Team.

Removing someone's access
  • To stop someone working in one database, open their lock icon (Permissions), untick that database, and click Done.
  • To stop them signing in, set Active to false with the pencil icon, or delete them from the RR Team page.

Database, module & company access

The role decides what a team member can do. Database, module and company access decides where. On both the New team member form and the Permissions dialog (the lock icon), you check the databases a team member may sign in to. Within each one, you tick the modules they may open and choose either All licensed companies or a specific set. The databases listed are the ones your own sign-in can reach.

  • Inventory and In Transit are ticked separately on each database, so someone can work In Transit in one database and not in another. RNV shows as Coming soon and can't be changed yet.
  • A database the team member doesn't have yet starts with the modules their role usually grants. Untick any they shouldn't have.
  • A module your company isn't licensed for shows Not licensed and stays closed.
  • Only the companies a team member is granted appear in their company picker on the Home page and the work pages.
  • Reconciliation totals reflect only the companies the team member is allowed to see — if someone's figures look off, check their company access first.
  • When a new company is licensed, team members set to All licensed companies pick it up automatically. Team members limited to a specific list must be updated to include it.

Editing, locking, and deactivating

From the Options column on the RR Team page:

IconAction
LockOpens Permissions for that person. Change their Role and their Databases (with modules and companies), then click Done to save. Closing the dialog with the X discards your changes.
PencilOpens Edit team member, titled with the person’s name. Edit Full Name, Email, App sign-in, Password and Active, then click Save Changes.
TrashOpens Delete team member. Click Delete to confirm. See Removing a team member.

In Edit team member:

  • Active is a list with two values, true and false. Setting it to false blocks sign-in without deleting the account. Use this when someone is on leave or has changed jobs.
  • Password sets a new password for the person (Minimum 8 characters · leave blank to keep). The field is hidden for someone who signs in through your identity provider, because that account can’t have a password.

Automatic inactivity management

RapidReconciler watches sign-in activity and helps keep your team list current on its own:

  • On the RR Team page each active member's row is tinted by recent activity: green when the account has been used in the last 6 months, amber at 6+ months, red at 12+ months. "Used" counts a sign-in or a password change, so a member who just set their password reads green even before their first sign-in. A member who has never signed in or set a password shows amber, so an account that was never set up stands out for review. The access review row on Home’s Team & Access card counts the members inactive 6+ months or 1+ year, until a current access review is recorded.
  • At 12 months of inactivity, RapidReconciler emails the team member a warning: sign in within 14 days or the account will be deactivated.
  • If they sign in during those 14 days, nothing changes — the warning clears and the account stays active.
  • If they don't, the account is deactivated automatically. Their history and settings are untouched; an administrator can turn the account back on at any time with the pencil icon (set Active to true).
Who is never deactivated automatically

Team members whose role includes administration (Administrator and Power User) are exempt, and so are service accounts on RapidReconciler’s exemption list. Because every administrator is exempt, the sweep can’t lock you out of your own site.

What a deactivated team member sees

If someone whose account was deactivated for inactivity tries to sign in, RapidReconciler tells them the account was deactivated for inactivity and to contact their administrator. Re-enabling them (pencil icon, Active set to true) lets them straight back in.

Passwords

A team member who signs in with a password sets it from the link in their invite email. You never see their password. The RR Team page has no control to re-send an invite. If someone’s link has expired or they can’t sign in:

  • They can click Forgot your password? on the sign-in page. RapidReconciler emails a reset link to the address on their team member record.
  • If that address is wrong, correct Email with the pencil icon first.
  • You can also type a new password for them in Password on the Edit team member dialog and pass it on.

Neither option applies to someone who signs in through your identity provider: the sign-in page hides Forgot your password? for their domain, and the dialog hides Password. Password length and complexity rules are covered in Complex Passwords.

App sign-in, and switching single sign-on off for one person

The Sign-in column on the RR Team page says how each person gets in: Password, or the name of your identity provider (for example Entra) when your company uses single sign-on. It is decided by the person's email domain, so everyone on a domain your company has federated signs in through the provider — a password from those accounts is refused.

Sometimes one identity needs a password anyway:

  • a service account that no person signs in as;
  • an employee not yet in your identity provider, so they can start work before IT adds them;
  • break-glass access while your provider is unavailable.

Open the team member with the pencil icon and tick Sign in with an RR password instead of … under App sign-in (the label ends with your provider’s name). The same choice is on the New team member form. Their row then reads Password with a bypasses marker beside it, so you can see at a glance who is outside your provider. If the account has never had a password, type one in Password in the same save: RapidReconciler refuses the bypass otherwise, because the account would have no way in.

This turns single sign-on off for that person

It is not a preference. That account no longer passes through your identity provider, so it is outside whatever your provider enforces — MFA, conditional access, device rules, and central deactivation when someone leaves. It is governed by RapidReconciler's own password rules instead, including the 90-day expiry. Grant it deliberately, and untick it when the reason has passed.

The tick box is hidden for a member whose domain is not federated: there is no single sign-on there to bypass, and that account already signs in with a password.

Common pitfalls

A team member can't see a company they should have access to

Module access opens the module; company access controls the data in it. Open the lock icon and confirm the right database is checked and the company is included (or that All licensed companies is on), then click Done.

Someone has a module they shouldn't — or is missing one

Modules are ticked per database. Open the lock icon, tick or untick the module under that database, and click Done. If the box is greyed out and says Not licensed, your company isn't licensed for it.

An Administrator can't see the analyst or accountant work

That is by design: each role sees only its own view. If the person also does the reconciliation work, make them a Power User.

A new hire never got their set-password email

Check the email address on the team member record and correct it with the pencil icon if needed. Check their Sign-in column too: someone on a single sign-on domain is not sent one unless you ticked the bypass. For a password account, the new hire can click Forgot your password? on the sign-in page to get a fresh link, since the page has no re-send control. See Passwords.

Removing a team member

Click the trash icon on the person’s row. The Delete team member dialog asks you to confirm and explains what deleting does: the account is deactivated and hidden from the list, and past audit references stay intact. Click Delete.

If you only need to stop someone signing in for a while, set Active to false with the pencil icon instead. The person stays on the list and you can turn them back on later.
