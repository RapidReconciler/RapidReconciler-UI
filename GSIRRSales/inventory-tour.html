The Key Concept · 01

Inventory never just drifts from the GL.

It drifts for a reason — and there are only a handful of them. The hard part has always been finding which one, buried across a dozen JD Edwards tables at month-end.

“A software solution for JD Edwards inventory, goods in transit and received-not-vouchered that streamlines reconciliations by replacing tedious manual tasks with automated processes and defined corrective actions.”

Picture a scale. Inventory on one side, the General Ledger on the other. Every transaction that posts to one side must post to the other, or the scale tips. When it tips, the explanation is hiding in one of the gaps between the layers — and each gap has its own fix.

Every transaction must post to each side equally — that’s how the scale stays in balance

Now let one posting land on inventory but miss the GL. The scale tips — and the question becomes which gap it slipped through.

A transaction hits, it’s off by 5. Which gap did it fall through?

Zoom into the transactional gap and the truth is plain — the posting hit Inventory for 10 but the GL for only 5. Fix: finish end-of-day processing, or write the JE.

RapidReconciler is the X-ray for that scale — it shows exactly which gap your variance slipped through, and hands each one to the person who can close it.
