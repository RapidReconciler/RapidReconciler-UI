Section 1

Solution overview

RapidReconciler reconciles JD Edwards perpetual inventory to the general ledger. Think of it as a balance scale with two stacks. The inventory side runs from item location balances (F41021) down to the item ledger (F4111). The GL side runs from account balances (F0902) down to account ledger detail (F0911). Whatever posts to one side should post to the other. Variances sit in the gaps between those layers.

RapidReconciler loads those tables every night, matches them document by document, and sorts every difference by how it gets fixed. Your analysts start from the reconciliation and drill to the document. They don't have to build the reconciliation first.

  • Built for JD Edwards: it reads your own tables and your own DMAAI setup.
  • Your data stays on your SQL Server, inside your network. GSI manages sign-in, releases and support centrally. The only reconciliation figures that leave your network are the ones sent with an AI question, at the AI level you choose (section 2.7), and you can switch AI off.
  • V8 is the current web application. Every number on screen comes straight from the reconciliation. AI explains the numbers; it never produces them.
Section 2

Core reconciliation functionality

2.1 Inventory-to-GL reconciliation at the transaction level

Available

Item ledger and inventory subledger to the GL.

The Inventory Reconciliation view shows, by company and period, the GL balance against the perpetual inventory balance and the out-of-balance between them. It splits that out-of-balance into six sources that add up to it exactly, and the same breakdown is available for every account. Each source opens the documents behind it. A document shows its item ledger amount, its GL amount and the difference, using the match key that fits its transaction type. Sales match on order number, manufacturing on work order, and so on.

2.2 Automated matching; unmatched, duplicate and out-of-balance transactions

Available

Every night RapidReconciler pairs each item ledger document with its GL lines. When the pairing finds an out-of-balance, it runs detectors that name the likely cause on the row:

  • Unmatched: item ledger activity with no GL batch (end of day), and GL entries with no item ledger side (manual entries).
  • Duplicate: duplicate sales relief and duplicate cardex relief on transfers.
  • Out of balance: GL that reached a different account than the DMAAI setup says it should, a GL date in a different period from the item ledger, non-stock lines, and A/P voucher variances on receipts.
  • Rows that carry an unasked question (a disclosure) are flagged in their own column. A difference is never hidden because it went unexplained.

2.3 Variance root-cause categorization

Available

RapidReconciler sorts variances by how they get fixed, so each one reaches the person who can fix it. Three sources need a journal entry from Finance. The other three clear when a process finishes or data is corrected, and a journal entry there would double-count. This is how your six categories map:

2.4 Corrective action workflow

Partial

Assigning, documenting, resolving and tracking exceptions.

  • Documenting Available Notes on any row, stored with the row, with who last changed them and when.
  • Resolving Available Mark rows worked one at a time or in bulk. Cardex variances clear with a reversible adjustment that you can undo, and each one is kept in an adjustment ledger. Self-service tools rebuild the GL or cardex copy from a date when JD Edwards has been corrected at the source.
  • Tracking Available Worked and not-worked filters, per-company period review and disposition, and the activity log (see 5.4).
  • Assigning Next release Hand any row to a named colleague, see the owner and hand-off date on the grid, and filter to "Assigned to me". It is built and in testing. The screens above and below show it.

2.5 Validation of quantities, costs, balances and current value

Available Depends on setup

Cardex Variance checks every item location's on-hand quantity and value (F41021) against the item ledger (F4111) that should produce it. Items are grouped by cost method (standard, average) and by quantity variance or amount variance, with branch/plant, location and lot on every row. Each item opens a worksheet that says what to do, routes the fix to JD Edwards or to RapidReconciler, and logs every adjustment. Full Perpetual Details values inventory at any period end, summarized by item or down to the lot.

RapidReconciler doesn't depend on as-of history from JD Edwards. At install it takes a snapshot of on-hand from F41021 while no transactions are in process, then works backwards through the item ledger to a balance forward. Every later balance is rolled forward from there, as the diagram below shows.

Cost comparisons follow your cost method. Frozen standard cost is checked against the cost records JD Edwards keeps, so a cost update that didn't run shows up as its own finding.

Worth confirming: which two figures your team compares today for item location against item summary availability, and from which JD Edwards screens or reports. GSI then shows that exact comparison instead of assuming it.

2.6 Daily, on-demand, month-end and year-end reconciliation

Available
  • Daily: data refreshes automatically every night.
  • On demand: pull an intraday refresh during close without waiting for the night run.
  • Month-end: each fiscal period has its own reconciliation, carry-forward, account roll-forward and sign-off.
  • Year-end: as-of balances at fiscal year end. After year-end close, older item ledger history can be purged by period to reclaim space, and the purge is logged.

2.7 AI-driven capabilities

Available
  • Variance explanation: every company on the reconciliation carries a short AI summary of what drives its out-of-balance. Click the out-of-balance figure and the RR Accounting Assistant runs a fuller analysis of that company.
  • Suggested resolutions: each summary ends with a recommended next step: the journal entry to book, the batch to post, or the process to finish. On a cardex item, Investigate explains why the variance happened and whether it is likely to come back.
  • Ask a question: the RR Accounting Assistant and the RR Analyst Assistant answer questions in plain language about the period on screen.
  • Anomaly detection: the detection is rule-based and repeatable (section 2.2), and so is the per-document what / why / how analysis. The AI explains what the rules found. It doesn't replace them.
  • A daily briefing on the analyst's Home, "Here's your day", says where the period stands and what needs attention first.
  • You control what data the AI sees: your administrator sets each AI feature to Off, Basic, Enhanced or Full, for each database, and every feature starts Off. At the lower levels, company identities stay masked. Requests go through GSI's service, so the AI provider's key never reaches a browser.
Section 3

Reporting and analysis

3.1 Historical as-of inventory balances by financial period

Available

Full Perpetual Details values inventory at any period end you have loaded, from the item ledger. It shows one row per item by default; Lot detail opens it up to location and lot. Every column filters from its header, and UOM conversions, frozen cost and GL class each have their own check one click away. The Account Roll Forward shows each account's beginning balance, activity and ending balance for every period, on both the inventory and GL sides.

3.2 Inventory-to-GL variance by account and organizational unit

Available

Every reconciliation figure is held by company, business unit, object and subsidiary, so variance reports group by account or by organizational unit without re-running anything. Each per-account view shows the six sources and their total, zeros included, so a row always ties.

3.3 Drill-down and filtering

Available
  • Company, business unit, object, subsidiary and currency filter the reconciliation and transactions.
  • Item, branch/plant, location and lot filter from the column headers on Full Perpetual Details, and every Cardex Variance row carries them.
  • From any card or account you drill to the documents, and from a document to its item ledger and GL lines.

3.4 Export capabilities and audit-ready evidence

Available
  • Available Grids export to Excel with the columns and order you have chosen. The Transactions export header names the filters that were on.
  • Available Audit report, per company and period, in Excel or PDF: the reconciliation by account, the variance documents behind each account, and the findings and work notes recorded against them.
  • Available Period Activity in PDF: every review, completion, cardex clearance and other recorded action for the period, with who and when.
  • Available A per-document Transaction Detail workbook, and a balancing-entry export for the journal entry Finance posts.
  • Available Evidence package in PDF: one file per company and period, with a cover page, the Audit report and the period's activity. One click in the Audit Center.

3.5 Data health and integrity reports

Available

The analyst's Data Health view re-runs these checks with every refresh. Each one shows green when it's clean or flagged with a count when it isn't, and each opens the flagged rows with a View list and an Excel download.

  • Model DMAAI Table: GL classes carrying inventory that are left out of your model DMAAI table (4152), with the amount they hold. The routing list beside it shows how each GL class routes to its accounts.
  • Account Roll Forward: confirms every period's GL and perpetual balances roll forward cleanly into the next, by account.
  • GL Class Integrity: items whose GL class on the branch/plant record disagrees with the GL class on the item location, so the same item can post to different accounts. Corrected at source in JD Edwards.
  • UOM Conversion Integrity: transactions in a unit of measure that has no conversion to the item's primary unit of measure, which misstates quantity and value.
  • Frozen Cost Integrity: item locations where the unit cost in use differs from the frozen standard cost, by location and lot, with the variance.
Section 4

Technical and integration

4.1 JD Edwards integration: approach, data sources and refresh

Available
  • Native read, no JD Edwards changes. RapidReconciler reads JD Edwards tables directly. It installs nothing inside JD Edwards and changes no JD Edwards object.
  • Sources: the item ledger (F4111), item location (F41021), account balances (F0902) and ledger (F0911), the DMAAI tables (F4095), sales order detail and history (F4211, F42119), purchase receipts (F43121), item and cost records, the data dictionary and the fiscal calendar.
  • JD Edwards databases: Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle and IBM i (AS/400). The installation guide covers the connection driver for each.
  • Refresh: SQL Server Integration Services on your server loads the data every night as a SQL Server Agent job. After the first load it pulls only what changed.

4.2 Your JD Edwards customizations

Available Depends on setup
  • RapidReconciler reads your own DMAAI setup, document types, order types and line types, so custom ones are handled the same way as standard ones.
  • Site-specific reconciliation rules go in a step reserved for your installation. Product upgrades never overwrite it.
  • Custom tables or programs that write their own inventory or GL entries need a review. If they write F4111 and F0911, RapidReconciler sees them like any other entry.
Worth confirming: your inventory-related customizations (custom document types, programs that post to F4111 or F0911, custom AAIs). Share a short list and GSI shows how each one appears in RapidReconciler.

4.3 In-transit and Received Not Vouchered reconciliation

Roadmap

In Transit (transfer orders between branches) and PO Receipts (received not vouchered) are licensed RapidReconciler modules with their own published guides. Their V8 screens are not released yet. In V8 today they appear on Home as coming soon. We'd rather say so up front.

  • Timing: the V8 In Transit and PO Receipts screens are planned for completion by the end of the first quarter of 2027.

4.4 Multi-company, multi-currency and intercompany

Available Depends on setup
  • Multi-company: any number of companies in one database, reconciled separately. Each user's access can be limited to the companies they work on.
  • Multi-currency: each company reconciles in its base currency. Transaction amounts can also be shown in a reporting currency, using the exchange rates already in JD Edwards.
  • Intercompany: each company's side of an intercompany transaction reconciles in that company.
Worth confirming: whether you also need intercompany eliminations or a consolidated multi-currency view. Neither is part of the inventory reconciliation today.

4.5 Architecture, hosting, performance and data volume

Available
  • Hosting: the database and data services run on your servers. Three layouts are supported: everything on one server, separate database and application servers, or a separate SSIS server. Standalone SQL Server, failover cluster instances and Availability Groups are all supported.
  • Server baseline: Windows Server 2019 or later, SQL Server 2019 or later (Standard edition or higher), 16 GB RAM, quad-core.
  • Data volume: plan about 100 GB of disk for each million item ledger records per month, which covers a year of history. The largest JD Edwards source we run today has about 7 million item ledger rows and 6.5 million GL ledger rows.
  • Performance: after the first full load, the nightly refresh is incremental. The first load of a new database can take several hours, which is why it's scheduled in a maintenance window.
Section 5

Controls and governance

5.1 Scheduled integrity checks, including DMAAI setup out of sync

Available
  • Every nightly refresh re-runs the integrity checks: cardex against location balances, GL batches, posting dates, and DMAAI routing.
  • DMAAI drift: every refresh re-checks your model DMAAI table (4152) against the GL classes actually carrying inventory. Model DMAAI Review lists any GL class left out of the model, with its items and amount, and your analyst marks each one Intended, Needs review or Fixed. If an excluded class's amount or items change after that review, the row is flagged ("amount changed", "items changed"), so an old review never passes silently.
  • Routing: a posting that reached a different account than your DMAAI setup says it should is flagged on the transaction itself (section 2.2).
  • Home shows the health of the last refresh and the reconciliation job, and says plainly when a refresh is late or did not run.

5.2 Audit trail and evidence retention

Available
  • The activity log records data loads, reconciliation runs, corrections, adjustments and their undo, sign-offs and dispositions, with who and when.
  • Every cardex adjustment is kept in an adjustment ledger and can be reversed.
  • Retention follows the data you keep loaded. History is removed only by a period purge that you run, and the purge is logged too.
  • JD Edwards stays your system of record. RapidReconciler's trail documents what was reviewed and corrected around it.

5.3 Role-based security and segregation of duties

Available
  • Roles decide which areas a person can use: reconciliation and analysis, DMAAI review, correction tools, administration.
  • Per-database rights, and within each database, all companies or a chosen list.
  • Sign-in: single sign-on with your identity provider (SAML or OpenID Connect), or RapidReconciler passwords under a complexity policy you set.
  • Access reviews: administrators record a periodic review of who has access, on a 30, 60 or 90-day cycle. Accounts unused for a year are warned and then deactivated.
  • Segregation: only administrators manage access. Analysts and accountants can't change their own rights or anyone else's.

5.4 Period-close status, approvals and sign-off

Available

The analyst marks each period reviewed once the transaction variance is worked. The accountant marks each company complete with a reason: accepted as immaterial, corrected with a posted journal entry, handed to the analyst for a source fix, or timing that will clear on its own. Each one is stamped with who and when, can be reopened, and appears in the Audit Center. Closing the period itself stays in JD Edwards.

Section 6

Implementation and adoption

6.1 Approach, resources and configuration

Available
  1. Prepare. Your IT team provisions the server(s), SQL Server and Integration Services, and applies the network rules. Your JD Edwards owner gathers the connection details. Both can happen at the same time. Server, network and JD Edwards connection requirements are in our Technical Requirements (provisioning) guide.
  2. Install. GSI installs the RapidReconciler database and data services remotely during a scheduled maintenance window.
  3. First load. The first full load of your JD Edwards history runs. For a large history it takes several hours.
  4. Configure and validate. Your analyst reviews and approves the DMAAI model, and together we confirm the first reconciliations tie to your JD Edwards balances.
  5. Go live. GSI hands administration to your RapidReconciler administrator, who adds users and sets their rights.

6.2 Training, documentation and JD Edwards upgrades

Available
  • Training is RapidReconciler University: searchable guides for each module (Inventory, A/P, Transfers, Administrators), with start-here paths by role, available to your whole team at any time.
  • Walkthroughs inside the app: the Accountant, Analyst and Administrator views each have a Walkthrough tab. It takes a new user through their work one step at a time, opens the screen each step is about, and uses your own data. Each step links to the guide that covers it. It works on every plan, with or without the AI assistant.
  • Help inside the app: each working screen has a help button that opens its RapidReconciler University guide in a panel beside the screen, so your team reads the guide without leaving the work. When the AI assistant answers a question, it links to the guide that covers it.
  • Help Desk: paste an error or describe a symptom and get step-by-step fixes your team can run themselves.
  • Release notes are published as changes ship.
  • JD Edwards upgrades: RapidReconciler reads standard JD Edwards tables and follows your DMAAI setup instead of hard-coding accounts, so most upgrades need no change on our side. Your site-specific rules survive our product upgrades too.

7.2 Support model and SLAs

RapidReconciler is built so your own team can find and fix most problems without opening a ticket.

On the administrator's Home view

  • Troubleshooting: one page checks the connections (database server to JD Edwards, the data services to GSI, days left on the server's security certificate), the data loads (the nightly refresh, the last JD Edwards import, the last reconciliation run and any open errors) and the database server (SQL Server Agent, disk space, the refresh job). Download diagnostics produces a workbook with the technical detail for your IT team, with a reference number to quote. From the same page, Connection Check runs from a user's own computer and shows exactly where a connection stops.
  • Data refresh: pull an intraday refresh during close without waiting for the night run.
  • Purge old data: remove history you no longer need after year-end, by period.
  • Team and access: RR Team (people, roles, rights and access reviews), Complex Passwords and Licensing.
  • Activity Log: recent system events, such as restarts, data reloads and refreshes, with when and who triggered them.

On the analyst's Tools tab and Account Roll Forward

  • Restart the report engine: if a report won't generate or looks out of date, restart it. It takes about 30 seconds and never changes your data.
  • Self-service repair: Reload cardex data and, from the Account Roll Forward, Reload GL data rebuild RapidReconciler's copy from a date once JD Edwards is correct. Period-end date correction fixes the fiscal calendar.

On the RapidReconciler help site

  • Help Desk: search a symptom or paste an error for step-by-step fixes, plus a log analyzer for the data-services log.

GSI support

  • How to reach us: email rrsupport@getgsi.com.
  • Response: within one business day (US Eastern). Most issues are resolved in that first response; otherwise, as soon as the issue has been clarified with you.
  • Monitoring: GSI monitors each installation's data services and refresh jobs centrally and can restart a service from its side.
  • SOC 2 Type 2: GSI's Genius AI and RapidReconciler systems are covered by a SOC 2 Type 2 report from an independent auditor. The current report and a bridge letter are available on request.
