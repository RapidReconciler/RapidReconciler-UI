Reload Cardex Data

When the perpetual cardex won’t roll cleanly and the variance won’t clear, this clears RapidReconciler’s copy of the cardex (F4111) and its transaction detail from a date you choose forward. JDE re-pulls it on the next scheduled refresh.

This is an administrator function. Contact your administrator to reload cardex data.
Couldn’t reach the data service —
Your JDE data is never changed, and this does not pull from JDE now. It clears RapidReconciler’s cardex copy from the date below; the cardex comes back on the next scheduled refresh. Refreshes load the JDE system, so they only run on the schedule.
