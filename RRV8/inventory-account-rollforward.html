Account Roll Forward

The GL and perpetual roll-forward by account and period — how each account’s beginning balance rolls to ending, whether GL and variance stay intact, and the cardex variance the per-item worklist reconciles to.
How this works GL & perpetual roll-forward by account

Account Roll Forward shows how each account’s balance rolls from one period to the next — on both the GL balance and the variance — and surfaces where a period didn’t roll cleanly.

  1. Pick the period you’re closing.
  2. Read the light. Green means GL and variance carried forward; red means a period broke — that’s the row to work.
  3. Find the break. A failed carry-forward is usually unposted GL batches in the prior period — post them in JDE and refresh.
  4. Drill to the items. The account-level cardex variance here is the sum of the per-item drift — open Cardex Variance to work it item by item.
