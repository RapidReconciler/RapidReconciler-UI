Cardex Variance

The item-focused cardex sync tool. Enter an item — or arrive from a Home Cardex Variance card — to review its RapidReconciler cardex position against JDE, then sync RapidReconciler with a logged, reversible Adjust Beginning Balance. Browse variance items on Home; sync them here.
How this works Item-focused cardex sync

Cardex Variance compares an item’s position in RapidReconciler (built from JDE) against JDE itself. When they differ, this page syncs RapidReconciler back to a validated JDE position — it never changes JDE.

  1. Focus an item. Type an item number (and branch) above, or click an item on Home’s Cardex Variance tab.
  2. Validate against JDE — the source of truth. Check quantity first, then amount. Confirm JDE is correct before you change anything in RapidReconciler.
  3. Open the cardex detail on a row to see the transactions behind the variance.
  4. Sync & verify. Adjust Beginning Balance is logged and reversible from the ledger below. The result confirms after the next refresh.
