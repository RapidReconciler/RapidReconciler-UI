How this works Per-item cardex drift worklist

Cardex Variance is the per-item companion to Account Roll Forward — items whose perpetual valuation didn’t roll cleanly. They sum to the account-level cardex variance.

  1. Group by pattern to see the worklist sorted into known causes, each with a short action plan.
  2. Open the cardex detail on a row to see the transactions behind its variance before you act.
  3. Confirm it’s real. A true JDE difference is fixed in JDE and clears on refresh; Adjust Beginning Balance is only for a load-time baseline artifact.
  4. Adjust & verify. Adjust Beginning Balance opens on the pattern’s likely fix — it’s logged and reversible from the ledger below.
