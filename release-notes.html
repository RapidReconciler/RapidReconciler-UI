What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

June 6, 2026

The Cardex variance list (opened from the Cardex card on the Reconciliation page) now splits the old combined notes field into clear Last Activity, Transaction Count, and Quantity On Hand columns, adds each item's Cost Level, and lets you sort by clicking any column heading. The reset date appears as a banner above the list, and the Excel download matches.

June 6, 2026

Model DMAAI Review now supports a row-by-row sign-off: mark each GL-class-to-account mapping OK or flag it for a change, add a note, and download a change request to hand to whoever maintains your accounts. The model sign-off is held until flagged mappings are resolved, and a new company filter narrows the tables for focused review.

The Administrator guide has been rewritten around your Home dashboard, with step-by-step walkthroughs and screenshots for People & Licensing, Accounting Set Up, Data, and Service Health — now one click away from the Administration link on Home.

June 6, 2026

The Data card can now purge old history in one step: pick a new start date from your fiscal calendar and every company is updated at once, with a clear warning that it's permanent after the next refresh and a reminder to back up the database first. Accounting Set Up now shows both the Model DMAAI Review and the DMAAI Analysis, and the admin cards are ordered setup-first.

June 6, 2026

New Model DMAAI Review on Home: see at a glance whether every inventory GL class maps to the right account in your model, which GL classes are excluded (and how many items that affects), and review the model and sign off when your accounting team has confirmed it. Corrections are made in JD Edwards and appear here after the next refresh.

June 5, 2026

The Service health card on Home now watches the data service for memory pressure and gives you a heads-up to restart before reports start slowing down or failing -- with an at-a-glance status and an optional details view.

June 5, 2026

Home now has a clear "Scope" section. The Working companies picker lets you tick exactly the companies you want to work in (not just one or all), and the database picker shows clean database names. Your choice carries through to your work pages.

June 5, 2026

- On Home you can now choose your working period (alongside database and company) in one place, and that choice carries into your work pages. - The "Am I ready to go?" check sits at the top and stays tidy — it collapses when everything's ready and expands when there's something to address.

June 5, 2026

- Home now lets you set your database, company, and working period in one place, and that choice carries into your work pages. - New administrators get a short guided setup that walks them through adding their team.

June 5, 2026

- The Team page is simpler to get around, with quick help for adding a teammate and choosing what they can see. - The Licensing page loads faster and lets you ask GSI about adding companies or seats. - The installation-prep form now confirms your JD Edwards source platform before you send it.

June 4, 2026

The Home page now adapts to your role — administrators get one-click access to data, service, and team controls, while everyone else gets a simple "ready to go" check before starting work. Sessions now time out after 30 minutes of inactivity instead of a fixed hour, so you won't be signed out mid-task.
