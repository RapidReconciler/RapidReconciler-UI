What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

June 4, 2026

The working pages now use the full width of your screen — the left navigation rail is gone, replaced by a compact top bar with the same module links, a connection indicator, and a Home button. On the Reconciliation page you can now break the variance down by company, and narrowing your view to specific companies moved to a single Company control in the top bar.

June 4, 2026

Signing in now takes you to a Home page built around your role. It points you straight to where your work starts, tells you at a glance whether anything needs your attention this period, and shows a connection indicator so you know right away if your network or VPN drops. Every page now has a Home link in the top bar to get you back.

June 3, 2026

The Licensing page now shows how many of your company licenses are in use. Adding or removing licensed companies is handled by GSI.

June 3, 2026

The administrator area is clearer: the sections are now Licensing, RR Team, and Utilities.

June 3, 2026

Administrators can now add team members directly in RapidReconciler. Each new teammate gets a secure email link to set their own password, and first-time administrators are guided to the setup page with a short getting-started checklist.

June 3, 2026

RapidReconciler go-live is smoother: your administrator now receives a welcome email with a secure link to set their own password and sign in - no temporary passwords to copy. The installation prep form now asks for your RapidReconciler administrator's name and email up front.

June 3, 2026

User role permissions now correctly take effect in the app: modules a user isn't granted are hidden from the sidebar, and admin-only actions no longer appear for users without permission.

June 3, 2026

User permissions are now role-based. Assign a user one role -- Administrator, Reconciliation Analyst, or Read-Only -- plus the databases they can access, instead of toggling each tab, function, and company individually.

June 3, 2026

Signing out of RapidReconciler now returns you to the full sign-in page instead of a pop-up, so it's clear you've been signed out and can sign back in cleanly.

June 2, 2026

The Connection Check now shows a live network map that pinpoints exactly where a connection breaks, with clearer labels, the address and port of each step, and a per-database view — so you can hand your IT the precise fix, or know when it is one for GSI.
