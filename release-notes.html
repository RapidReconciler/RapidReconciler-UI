What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

July 20, 2026

Account Roll Forward now gives clearer corrective guidance. The corrective-actions panel shows the two GL-side steps to work directly (request the balance repost, then reload the GL), and explains that variance-side differences resolve automatically on the next refresh — with a clear path to support if one ever persists.

July 19, 2026

- The administrator screens now share one consistent, cleaner look. - The Report Engine card explains what it does before you restart it. - Dedicated help for the maintenance tools: correcting a period-end date, reloading GL, and reloading cardex each open their own how-to. - The AI Assistant "& Your Data" help now leads with the reassurance and an at-a-glance data-flow.

July 18, 2026

Administrator help answers are now drawn from the live help center, so questions about renewing licenses and adding users return accurate, up-to-date steps. Licensing details now read consistently across the product and the help docs, and the Administrator guides have been refreshed.

July 17, 2026

- The Administrator home is redesigned so the things you act on (licensing, your team, recent activity) come first, with routine status tucked into a compact strip. - The admin AI assistant now answers licensing, renewal, user, and password questions from the built-in help, and offers suggested questions you can click. - The analyst "Overview" tab is now "Data Health," and the configuration-check tables use shorter, clearer column headers. - Readability and layout cleanups across the admin screens.

July 16, 2026

- The month-end reconciliation view now leads with the companies you can act on, instead of ones that are waiting on an analyst; those show a clear "waiting on an analyst re-roll" status. - The assistant no longer offers to "clear these variances" when there's nothing for you to clear. - The configuration checks (UOM, Frozen Cost, GL-Class) now let you review the flagged items on screen before exporting them to Excel.

July 16, 2026

- The built-in AI assistant now answers more consistently across screens — the same reconciliation guidance drives the Home briefing, the roll-forward helper, the As-Of explainer, and the journal-entry summary. - Small on-screen text (hints, card subtext, control labels) is a little larger and easier to read.

July 16, 2026

- The Cardex Variance view has a cleaner, more colorful layout: clearer titles, aligned figures, and a bar that shows each variance's size at a glance. - The analyst assistant now answers cardex-variance questions accurately and reflects the figures currently on screen. - Cardex variance clears now appear in the Audit Center.

July 15, 2026

Cardex Variance now walks you through syncing a stale on-hand variance to the cardex in one pass: preview the projected roll-forward, clear the variance, and approve it. Cleared items move into their own list on the home screen and can be reopened if you need to reverse the change.

July 15, 2026

Cardex Variance workbench: a guided flow to validate an item against JDE, then record a source correction or sync RapidReconciler to the JDE figure.

July 14, 2026

Analyst Transaction Details refinements: a cleaner scope bar with the total variance anchored on the right, the analyzer and details grid collapsed by default, an AI-drafted findings note sized to its content, and the model account description shown alongside the inventory account in the routing grid.
