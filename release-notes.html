What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

August 9, 2026

Figures that appear on more than one screen now come from a single calculation, so the Data Health summary, the day's briefing and the Model DMAAI Review page always state the same numbers. Counts also say what they are counting: items and stock rows are named separately instead of one being reported as the other. The Account Roll Forward summary on Home now reports accounts that could not be evaluated instead of showing them as clean.

August 9, 2026

The account roll-forward no longer reports baseline rows as unevaluated. A baseline is the opening snapshot and has no prior period to roll forward from, so it was never a gap in the check — the page now says so, and stops prompting for a GL reload that would not have changed anything.

August 9, 2026

Every analyst page now has a help panel listing what the terms on that screen mean — linked transactions, the Rel and Orig columns, cardex variance, the excluded-class markers — the same definitions the assistant works from.

August 9, 2026

The analyst assistant now works from a single definition of the terms on your screens, so asking what something means gives you this product's answer in JD Edwards terms — and the same definitions apply on every analyst page rather than one of them.

August 9, 2026

The analyst assistant now answers questions about the terms on screen using this product's own definitions, in JD Edwards document terms, rather than reaching for similar-sounding accounting concepts.

August 9, 2026

Ask the analyst assistant what a linked pair is and it now answers in JD Edwards terms — the documents that make up one business event, with a transfer's shipping and receiving orders as the example — instead of describing accounting entries.

August 9, 2026

The account roll-forward now has its own panel at the top of Data Health, so you can open it whether or not it needs attention — previously a clean roll-forward collapsed into a summary line with nowhere to click. The AI reads also no longer refer to you in the third person.

August 9, 2026

At the Full AI level, the Frozen Cost check now explains what the flagged items actually are — components rolled without a frozen standard, or a partial roll — which branches they sit in, and how much stock is being valued at zero, rather than reporting a count.

August 9, 2026

The Data Health tab's check list is now headed "Integrity Review", which says what the list is rather than repeating the summary above it.

August 9, 2026

The Data Health summary now tells you how many categories need your attention rather than repeating the first one, and the model table's warning marker is amber like every other one on the page.
