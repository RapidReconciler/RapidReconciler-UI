What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

June 26, 2026

- The sign-in page has a fresh layout with a "what's new" panel and quick links to our SOC 2 reports. - Each administrator tool - data refresh, purge old data, reload GL, reload cardex, review schedule, and period-end correction - now opens on its own page with built-in help. - Licensing now shows how many days remain on your license, flags an upcoming expiry, and lets you request a renewal invoice in one click. - The RR Team page now shows each member's sign-in method (password or single sign-on).

June 26, 2026

The in-app help panel now opens beside your work instead of covering it, and jumps to the relevant section when you click a control. Admin pages use clearer "team member" wording, the RR Team page shows at-a-glance activity (green/amber/red) with an auditor-ready access export, and sign-in now supports single sign-on for SSO-enabled organizations.

June 11, 2026

Account Roll Forward now walks you through every out-of-balance: each red light shows the cause, the exact step to fix it, and a one-click jump to that account's full history. DMAAI Analysis shows at a glance whether your model is approved.

June 10, 2026

Home now shows your RapidReconciler database version and a tidier scope panel, the Account Roll Forward and analyst pages respect the companies you've selected, and the Administrator guide has been refreshed.

June 10, 2026

- The Home page is reorganized around a single Scope panel: choose your database, companies, and period in one place, with at-a-glance install details (data currency, history range, environment). - Each Home section now shows an at-a-glance status, so you can see whether Administrator, Analyst, Accountant, or Support items need attention before you dig in. - Cardex Variance now uses a per-company tolerance you set on the page: the status stays green while variance is within your threshold, instead of flagging every small difference.

June 10, 2026

Inventory reconciliation now includes a balancing-entry worksheet, clearer transaction-variance direction, at-a-glance insights for the period close, and company-scoped analyst views.

June 8, 2026

Updated the Administrator help to match the latest app: - Managing users now covers roles and email invitations - new users get a secure link to set their own password instead of an admin-assigned one. - The password, company, and licensing guides were refreshed to match the current Home and Administration screens, including the read-only company-license usage view.

June 8, 2026

The Home page header was refreshed: it now shows the GSI logo, the page title is centered, and the database selector is a smaller, tidier control.

June 8, 2026

Help Desk & RR University updates: - Comparing RR to JDE: new "Negative on-hand in RR / positive in JDE" guide covering the common causes (timing, a missing receipt, or 3PL/in-transit branches) and how to investigate. - Excel export failures: now covers the case where an export returns a 500 error because the database is set to read-only. - No data after login: now covers a recently-moved server or changed IP, with a ready-to-send note to RR support.

June 8, 2026

Two inventory fixes: - The Reconciliation and Transactions pages now open on the same companies you selected on Home — including when Home is set to all companies. - Quantity columns on the Cardex detail (such as adjusted quantity and quantity on hand) no longer show a currency symbol — they're counts, not amounts.
