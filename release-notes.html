What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

June 8, 2026

The Home page got a polish pass: - Each Administration section now collapses to a clean header that shows its status at a glance (for example "Roll-forwards intact" or "Model DMAAI review pending"), and opens to the full tools with one click. - The Administration heading shows an overall health color — green, amber, or red — reflecting the sections beneath it. - A new "What's New" panel surfaces the latest release notes right on Home, with the database picker moved up beside the page title.

June 8, 2026

RapidReconciler now shows inventory amounts in each company's own currency symbol. When you view companies that use different currencies together, the page asks you to narrow to a single currency before showing combined totals, so a total never mixes currencies.

June 8, 2026

Inventory pages: - Reconciliation, Transactions, and Perpetual now share a consistent contributors breakdown (Business unit / Object / Subsidiary, plus Branch plant on Perpetual) you can click to narrow the grid. - Perpetual report buttons show a count badge when a report has items to review in the current scope. - The company selection now filters every surface consistently, and a brief connection blip no longer flickers the status. - A shared footer was added to the inventory pages.

The Export Analyzer gained a new Transaction Detail diagnosis for duplicate shipments on the same order line, with the inventory-adjustment fix.

June 7, 2026

Inventory pages refresh: - Reconciliation, Transactions, and Perpetual now share a consistent header layout, with the by-period trend shown as a line chart. - The company selection and the active period now stay in sync across all three pages. - New Excel reports on the Transactions page: Variance by Order Type and Variance by Doc Type.

June 7, 2026

Home and inventory polish: - The Home RR University and Help Desk cards now show results in a drawer as you type, right on Home — click a result to open it, no page jump. University results are scoped to what you have access to. - The Reconciliation, Transactions, and Perpetual pages now share one consistent header, with the company picker in the same place on all three.

June 7, 2026

Home and guidance improvements: - The Home support cards now search RR University or start a Help Desk fix right from Home — type a question and it opens the page with results ready. - Admin and review pages each have a collapsible "How this works" panel with the quick steps for that task. - The Administration section on Home collapses to keep the dashboard tidy.

June 7, 2026

Cardex Variance worklist improvements: - Open any item to see the cardex transactions behind its variance, and export them to Excel. - Items are grouped into plain-language patterns, each with a short action plan. - Adjust Beginning Balance now opens on the most likely correction for the item.

June 7, 2026

Cardex Variance is now a live page: a per-item worklist of items whose perpetual valuation didn't roll cleanly, grouped by account and searchable, that ties to the Account Roll Forward. You can correct a row in place with a logged, reversible "Adjust Beginning Balance" (Clear / Zero / Manual). The page now preloads in the background so it opens instantly.

June 7, 2026

Home page updates: - New Utilities section with self-service "Reload GL Data" and "Reload Cardex Data" to clear and rebuild data when a roll-forward won't balance -- the rebuild happens on the next scheduled data refresh. - Admin task cards now shade green, amber, or red so you can see status at a glance. - Clearer layout: a page banner, separate Working Companies and Working Period cards, a dedicated Support section, and a refreshed footer. - A data refresh can no longer be started while one is already running.

June 7, 2026

- New Account Roll Forward review on the home page (Inventory review): the GL and perpetual roll-forward by account and period, with a clear intact / needs-attention read and an Excel export. - The Inventory review card now shows a green/red validation light at a glance. - The database picker now sits above the administration tasks it applies to.
