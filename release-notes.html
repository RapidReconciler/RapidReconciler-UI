What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

August 28, 2026

The transaction-variance grid now marks rows that carry a question the analysis did not answer, and says what it is. Where one order's rows were reported on several different cards, any one of them now names the others and the amount sitting on them, so working a single card no longer leaves the rest of the order unexamined.

The Completion Not Journaled card's notes were reworded. Two of them described sample data; they now tell you what to check in your own.

August 27, 2026

The web address in the help documentation now matches the address you sign in to. Pages that told you to browse to, bookmark, or allow the old address have been corrected, and the Connection Check tests the address you actually use.

August 27, 2026

Home now shows how current your data is, for everyone. A pill in the bottom bar reads "Data as of" and the date it was last pulled from JD Edwards, and turns red if the last refresh did not finish -- so you can tell at a glance whether you are reconciling against last night's figures or against a refresh that failed days ago. It used to be visible only to administrators.

August 27, 2026

The assistant can now point you at the matching help article on the Cardex Variance and Transactions pages, the way it already does on Home. Links only ever come from a fixed list of real help articles.

August 27, 2026

Fixed several places where a status message or badge stayed on screen after it should have cleared. The clearest of these was on Reload Cardex and Reload GL, where the "a job is running" warning was showing at all times instead of only while a job was actually running.

August 26, 2026

Four fixes on the analyst surfaces. The DMAAI model review no longer leaves "Reviewing the model..." on screen after it has finished reviewing. Audit Center entries no longer repeat the company and period that are already shown above them. The re-roll page now tells you when it could not check whether the database is busy, instead of leaving the button enabled with no explanation. And a recorded finding can now be discarded, with an Undo, rather than being permanent once written.

August 26, 2026

Two fixes on the reconciliation surfaces. Your own journal entries now appear on the period Audit Center and in the report it exports — until now they were filtered out whenever a period was selected, which is always on that screen. And the DMAAI decisions panel now says plainly when an analysis simply has not run yet, instead of reporting that your earlier decisions could not be loaded. Those are different situations, and only one of them is a problem.

August 25, 2026

The AI Assistant page now shows the plan you actually own. It previously reported that the assistant was not part of your plan, and held all three features at Off, even for customers on the top tier. Your plan is also shown on the Home screen again.

The Licensing card on Home now tells you how many company licences the database you are working in uses, rather than a total spanning every database on your account -- so it agrees with the Licensing page. Its colour now matches its status: green when the licence is current, amber when renewal is close, red once expired. Expired licences previously looked the same as healthy ones.

Team and Access no longer reports "Access up to date" when it could not read your review state. It says the state is unavailable instead, and still shows anyone inactive for 30 days or more.

August 25, 2026

The GL Class Integrity report now leads with the two GL classes it compares — the branch class and the location class, side by side. The computed mismatch label and the item-master class column have been removed; the check is about the branch and the location disagreeing, and those two values now sit together at the front of the table and of the Excel export.

August 24, 2026

The analyst day brief now follows the period you select on the Transaction Variance chart. Clicking a period re-reads the brief for that period instead of leaving the previous period's wording on screen.
