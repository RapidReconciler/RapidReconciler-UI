What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

September 12, 2026

Housekeeping only: a large amount of unreachable code left over from the retired demo mode has been removed from the application. There is no change to how anything behaves.

September 12, 2026

RapidReconciler now signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, and warns you two minutes beforehand with a Stay signed in button so you are never dropped mid-task without notice. Separately, if your sign-in itself reaches its expiry you are given the same two-minute warning to save your work. The sign-in page now explains which of the two happened.

September 12, 2026

Three analysis reports on the Inventory As Of page — Frozen Cost, GL Class integrity and UOM Conversions — are reachable again, and the Inventory Transactions grid regains its Update button for editing several selected rows at once. The As Of reports stay disabled until a single company is in scope, with the reason shown beside them.

September 12, 2026

The Installation Prep guide now covers secure web gateways and zero-trust access products such as Zscaler Private Access, Netskope, Prisma Access, Umbrella and Forcepoint. It explains why an outbound firewall rule does not by itself grant your users access, what your gateway administrator needs to set up, and why adding a RapidReconciler database later also means adding a port. Two related behaviours are covered: a gateway can block or truncate an installer download, and it can drop the JMS port as non-web traffic even when your firewall permits it. The pre-install checklist and the "what to send GSI" list now ask whether such a product is in your path.

September 11, 2026

Each transaction-variance card now shows how many rows are behind it without being opened, alongside the period and the variance amount.

September 11, 2026

The Transaction Variance and Overview screens now open with their cards collapsed, so a period's work reads as a short list you expand one item at a time. A card you open stays open while you work in it.

September 11, 2026

The sign-in page now explains more clearly what to do when an account is managed by your organization's identity provider, and shows the single sign-on button alongside that explanation. Administrator screens no longer offer a password box for accounts that sign in through an identity provider, and the sign-in option is hidden where there is nothing to change.

September 10, 2026

Exported Variance Source Detail workbooks now name the database they were taken from in the title band, instead of a generic placeholder.

The AI plan shown on Home is now the plan your organisation actually holds. Previously anyone who was not an administrator saw the top tier regardless, and the pill now reads "Not enabled" when the assistant has not been switched on for your server.

Administrators can set how each person signs in to the application from the New User and Edit User screens.

* the SSO-bypass checkbox stretched to full width and pushed its own label out of both modals, and the guard against that was overridden by source order

Two fixes, both found after the first push: one by CI, one by the owner looking at the screen.

1. THE BYPASS CHECKBOX IS FULL-WIDTH IN BOTH USER MODALS.

`.form-field input, .form-field select` is written for text inputs -- width:100%, 14px, an 8px/10px padding and a border -- and it was also hitting the SSO-bypass checkbox, which sits inside `.form-field` divs (#js-edit-signin-block and #js-nu-signin-block). The box stretched across the whole row, pushed its own <span> label off the right edge, and gave both modals a horizontal scrollbar. On screen it reads as a lone tick floating in the middle of the field with the sentence clipped somewhere to the right.

⚠ THE DEFENCE WAS ALREADY THERE AND DID NOTHING. `.signin-bypass-toggle input` at :275 sets `width: auto` for exactly this reason. Both selectors are specificity (0,1,1), so the LATER one wins -- and `.form-field input` is 130 lines further down. A guard that is written, looks correct, and is silently beaten by source order is worse than no guard, because it stops the next person looking.

Scoped with :not([type="checkbox"]) rather than out-specifying it, so a future checkbox in any .form-field is correct by default instead of by remembering.

⚠ AND MY FIRST READING OF THIS WAS WRONG. Asked whether V8 had the same defect as the console, I grepped for lines containing BOTH `input` and `width` and concluded V8 was clean. Multi-line CSS rules defeat that -- the declaration sits four lines below the selector. Same class of miss as a `git grep "|| 'demo'"` that cannot match `||'demo'`. Re-measured by parsing every rule whose selector mentions `input` and printing its body, which found it immediately.

2. Tools/test-demo-mode-unreachable.js stripped block comments with the naive regex, and Tools/test-comment-stripper-safety.js (UI-170) caught it in CI.

That expression pairs a `/*` inside a STRING or REGEX LITERAL with the wrong delimiter; measured on sidebar.js it removes 48% of the file. In this gate specifically that is a FALSE PASS risk, not a false fail: A3 and A4 scan RRV8 source for `?mode=` reads and `|| 'demo'` fallbacks, so a stripper that deletes real code makes them pass because the evidence is gone.

Replaced with the line-comments-only form the repo sanctions, /^[ \t]*\/\/.*$/gm, which matches only a comment that OPENS a line so a `//` inside a URL survives. Sufficient here because every comment this gate has to ignore is a line comment. Both gates green after the change: the meta-test passes and the demo gate still reports 11 assertions plus its mutation control.

⚠ Worth stating plainly: the whole-tree parsecheck I ran locally before the first push reported 187/187 and could not have caught this. It builds its list from `git ls-files`, which lists TRACKED files, and the three new Tools/*.js files were still untracked. A local gate that silently skips new files is a gate that passes hardest exactly when you have added something.

September 10, 2026

The Transactions grid can filter by worked status again. A control in the Details header switches the grid and its subtotals between all rows, just the rows you have worked, and the rows still outstanding.

September 9, 2026

If your organization uses Zscaler, the Agent Upgrade guide now has a section for it. At two customer sites, users could not see their data until their accounts were granted access to a RapidReconciler application segment in Zscaler -- everything else was configured correctly. The section shows the exact line to look for in your browser, explains why it affects some users and not others, and gives your Zscaler administrator the three things to set up. It also flags something easy to miss later: each RapidReconciler database is served on its own port, so adding a database means adding a port.

The "no data after logging in" troubleshooting page has a matching step. It walks you through checking your browser for a blocked connection and gives you a ready-made sentence to send to your IT team, so a permissions change on their side does not turn into a long investigation.
