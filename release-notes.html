What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

July 1, 2026

- Reconciliation: each variance source now opens a quick-glance detail view with a one-click Excel export, plus a plain-English AI panel that reads the period and answers questions. - Transactions summarize by account and type (Sales, Purchasing, Manufacturing, Inventory). - Audit reports (Excel and PDF) now include an AI-written plain-English summary on the cover. - Clearer message when reports are briefly slow. - Accountant home now has a direct "Open Reconciliation" action.

June 30, 2026

- Reconciliation now opens as an AI-assisted close: ask about the period in plain English, and assemble a period-end journal entry (exportable to Excel) in a click. - Reconcile one company at a time, and open the perpetual (As Of) detail straight from the balance. - The administrator home cards now show live detail at a glance -- team activity, report-engine health with a one-click restart, and a recent-activity feed.

June 30, 2026

The Administrator home is now a cleaner, at-a-glance dashboard: - Your AI Assistant, licence, and data status sit front and centre. - Licensing shows the renewal date on a calendar with a clear days-left countdown. - A single Data card tells you whether your data is current, how much storage runway you have, and whether any calendar corrections are needed.

June 30, 2026

- The Home assistant is clearer and more accurate: every item leads with the responsible role and company, current and historical periods read correctly, and every company in scope is covered. Click an item for details — or, on a closed period, a summary of what happened and whether it was resolved. - The "Inventory Out of Balance by Period" chart now visually separates current periods from historical ones. - New role-based dashboard views — switch between the Administrator, Analyst, and Accountant views right from the dashboard.

June 30, 2026

- Refreshed the sign-in screen with a cleaner, AI-forward design. - Reimagined Home around a built-in AI assistant that reads your reconciliation and tells you where to look first, in plain English and prioritized for your role. - Added an "Inventory Out of Balance by Period" chart; click any period for the assistant's read of it. - Streamlined Home to show just what needs attention, with a "Show all" toggle to see everything.

June 29, 2026

Account Roll Forward is now a guided, step-by-step workflow - see what's off, pick the right corrective action, and verify against the roll-forward report. The Excel export is audit-quality and highlights the accounts that don't reconcile.

June 29, 2026

The AI Assistant settings page is clearer — collapsible sections, and a one-tap check that confirms the assistant is reachable.

June 29, 2026

Carry forward now opens a full detail page like the other variance sources, showing the prior period's composition that rolled into the current period.

June 28, 2026

- Inventory Reconciliation: the variance breakdown is now a compact, collapsible panel — each source shows a clear status (green when there's nothing to do, amber when it needs attention) and opens to its own detail page with a sortable, exportable grid. - Every page now reflects only the database you've selected, with no mix-ups when you switch databases. - Refreshed administrator "Start Here" guidance and cleaner page headers.

June 28, 2026

- Home now reflects the database you select: each database shows only its own status, and the role bands, Today's To Do, status dots, and working period stay in agreement when you switch databases. - The Administrator status bar now matches its cards (it no longer reads green when something needs attention), and Today's To Do agrees with the bands. - The Activity Log is now searchable and filterable. - Admin pages: the redundant 'Back to Home' button at the bottom was removed; the one in the header remains.
