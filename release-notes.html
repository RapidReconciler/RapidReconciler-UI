What’s new in RapidReconciler.

Newest changes appear at the top — each entry is auto-published from a merge to main. Spot something missing or want context on a fix? rrsupport@getgsi.com.

June 29, 2026

Carry forward now opens a full detail page like the other variance sources, showing the prior period's composition that rolled into the current period.

June 28, 2026

- Inventory Reconciliation: the variance breakdown is now a compact, collapsible panel — each source shows a clear status (green when there's nothing to do, amber when it needs attention) and opens to its own detail page with a sortable, exportable grid. - Every page now reflects only the database you've selected, with no mix-ups when you switch databases. - Refreshed administrator "Start Here" guidance and cleaner page headers.

June 28, 2026

- Home now reflects the database you select: each database shows only its own status, and the role bands, Today's To Do, status dots, and working period stay in agreement when you switch databases. - The Administrator status bar now matches its cards (it no longer reads green when something needs attention), and Today's To Do agrees with the bands. - The Activity Log is now searchable and filterable. - Admin pages: the redundant 'Back to Home' button at the bottom was removed; the one in the header remains.

June 28, 2026

- You now stay signed in across page reloads — refreshing no longer sends you back to the sign-in screen. - Reference documents print cleanly from the app, with correct page breaks and nothing cut off.

June 28, 2026

Home's System Health card now opens focused pages — Data Service (status, memory, restart), AI Assistant (opt-in AI help with per-feature controls), and Activity Log — each with a review reminder. The role lanes on Home now shade green, amber, or red by live status.

June 27, 2026

- The V8 Home page now opens with a tidy "Set Context" bar and a "Today's To Do" summary of what needs attention, grouped by role. - Reference documents now have a Print button and print cleanly (correct page breaks, nothing cut off). - Reload Cardex now suggests a reload date when recent end-of-day activity is detected, with a "remind me in 30 days" option, and both Reload GL and Reload Cardex are paused while a data refresh is running.

June 27, 2026

- Refreshed sign-in page: clearer branding, a what's-new feed, and links to explore more from GSI. - Review Job Schedule now lets you confirm your nightly refresh time with a simple reminder, shows the schedule front-and-center, and sends a change request to your on-site DBA when the timing needs to move.

June 27, 2026

The Administrator area gained several review aids: - RR Team: sign off that your user list is correct on a 30/60/90-day schedule; the access report now includes a sign-off log for auditors. - Complex Passwords: set a 3- or 6-month reminder (or turn it off) to acknowledge your password-rule setup. - Correct a Period-End Date: the page now checks for a stale date the moment it opens, and the Home dot shows green when there's nothing to fix. - Purge Old Data: a clearer "when to purge" recommendation, a reminder you can snooze, and a history of every start-date change. Home status dots now reflect each of these at a glance, and the sign-in page no longer says "all systems operational" when only sign-in was checked.

June 27, 2026

The Purge Old Data page now recommends when a purge is worth considering, based on disk space and how much history you're keeping, with a reminder you can set for 1, 3, or 6 months. It also keeps a history of every start- date change - who made it, the old and new dates, when it changed, and when the purge actually ran.

June 26, 2026

Complex password rules can now be turned on per company. Open Administrator > People & Licensing > Complex Passwords to require stronger passwords for the companies that need them - one at a time or all at once. Because a person can work in more than one company, the strongest rule wins: anyone who can reach a company that requires complex passwords will be asked for one. Single sign-on users are unaffected.
